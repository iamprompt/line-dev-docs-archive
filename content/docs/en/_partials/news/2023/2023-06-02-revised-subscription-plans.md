As announced on [October 31, 2022](/news/2022/10/31/revise-subscription-plans/) and [April 12, 2023](/news/2023/04/12/revise-subscription-plans/), we've revised the LINE Official Account subscription plans as of June 1, 2023. In this revision, the pricing for each plan remain unchanged, but the number of messages that can be sent for free has been changed. The revised plans apply to Japan only.

For more information about the revision of LINE Official Account subscription plans, see [Important: LINE Official Account new plan structure and end of prorated billing](https://www.linebiz.com/jp/news/20221031/?utm_source=line&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=oa_ck_oam_developers_230419){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"} (only available in Japanese) in LINE for Business.
