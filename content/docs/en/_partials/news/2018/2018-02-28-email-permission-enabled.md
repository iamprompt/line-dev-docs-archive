Now you can request users who log in using LINE Login to grant your app the permission to get their email address. To do so, you first need to apply for the permission in the LINE Developers Console.

For more information, see the sections below in [Integrating LINE Login with your web app](/docs/line-login/integrate-line-login/).

- [Applying for email permission](/docs/line-login/integrate-line-login/#applying-for-email-permission)
- [Scopes](/docs/line-login/integrate-line-login/#scopes)
