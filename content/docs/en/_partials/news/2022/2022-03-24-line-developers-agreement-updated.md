### What is LINE Developers Agreement?

[LINE Developers Agreement](https://terms2.line.me/LINE_Developers_Agreement?lang=en){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"} is an agreement regarding the terms of use for SDKs or APIs provided by LINE. [Developers](/docs/line-developers-console/overview/#developer) must agree to this agreement before using the SDKs or APIs.

LINE will continue to improve the quality of its services for its customers. Thank you for your understanding.
