From now on, LINE Developers News will offer selected articles in [Chinese](https://developers.line.biz/zh-hant/news/){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"} (in addition to English and [Japanese](https://developers.line.biz/ja/news/){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"}), making it easier for Chinese-speaking developers to stay up-to-date on LINE development.

To change your language setting to Chinese, scroll to the bottom of the page and select "繁體中文" (Traditional Chinese) from the language menu in the page footer:
