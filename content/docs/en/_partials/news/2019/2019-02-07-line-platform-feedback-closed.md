We will archive the [LINE Platform feedback repository](https://github.com/line/line-platform-feedback){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"} on GitHub on February 15, 2019.

Please send your questions about the LINE Platform to the [Q&A](https://www.line-community.me/en/questions){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"} page of the LINE developers community site. Like many technical forums, you can post and answer questions freely, and leverage the development know-how accumulated within the developer community for the LINE Platform.
