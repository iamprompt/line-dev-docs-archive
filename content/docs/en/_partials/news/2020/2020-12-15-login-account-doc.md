As [announced on October 13, 2020](/news/2020/10/13/login-with-business-account/), you can currently use these accounts to log in to [LINE Developers Console](/console/).

- LINE account
- Business account

Regarding the available features when you log in to LINE Developers Console using each account, we added explanations to [Log in to LINE Developers](/docs/line-developers-console/login-account/). This document also explains how to [link your business account with your LINE account](/docs/line-developers-console/login-account/#link-business-account-with-line-account).

For more information on LINE Developers Console, see the LINE Developers Console [Overview](/docs/line-developers-console/overview/).

LINE will continue to improve the quality of the LINE Developers site and its documentation.
