Emergency maintenance of the LINE Developers site was carried out on the following dates and times.

We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.

### Date and time of maintenance

- LINE Developers site

  - November 9, 2020 around 17:15 - 18:46 (UTC+9)
- LINE Developers Console

  - November 9, 2020 around 17:15 - 22:07 (UTC+9)

### Impact of maintenance

From the time stated above, you weren't able to browse the LINE Developers site ([https://developers.line.biz/](https://developers.line.biz/){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"}) or log in to the LINE Developers Console ([https://developers.line.biz/console/](https://developers.line.biz/console/){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"}).
