In order to improve the quality of the [LINE Developers Console](/console/) for our users, we've added a feedback form to the LINE Developers Console.

If you would like to report any bugs in the LINE Developers Console or would like to leave any suggestions, scroll down to the bottom of the page and click the feedback form icon.

![LINE Developers Console feedback form icon](/media/news/console-feedback-form-icon.png){className="[\"border\",\"w-fix-400\"]"}

After clicking the icon, the feedback form will appear. You can then leave any feedback you may have for us.

![LINE Developers Console feedback form](/media/news/console-feedback-form.png){className="[\"border\",\"w-fix-400\"]"}

We read every single feedback message, but we can't respond to questions asked through the feedback form. To get technical support, use the [inquiry form](https://contact-cc.line.me/serviceId/11556/){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"}.

We greatly appreciate your feedback and we are committed to improving your experience using our developer products.
