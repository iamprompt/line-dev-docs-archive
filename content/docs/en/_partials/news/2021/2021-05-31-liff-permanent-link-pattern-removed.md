As announced on [March 01, 2021](/news/2021/03/01/liff-replace-mode-discontinuation-announcement/), **Replace (Backward compatibility mode)** in **Method for converting additional information in the LIFF URL** has been discontinued on the [LINE Developers Console](/console/).

Currently, because all LIFF and LINE MINI App URLs are opened with **Concatenate**, we've deleted the **Method for converting additional information in the LIFF URL** setting on the LINE Developers Console.

This deletion doesn't affect the operation of LIFF apps or LINE MINI Apps.

For **Concatenate** specifications, see [Create a secondary redirect URL](/docs/liff/opening-liff-app/#setting-second-redirect) in the LIFF documentation.

The [**Example code for converting URL generated by "Concatenate" to URL of "Replace (Backward compatibility mode)" specification**](/docs/liff/handle-replace-mode-url/), which was provided as a temporary measure to handle the discontinuation of **Replace (Backward compatibility mode)**, will be removed at the end of June 2021.
