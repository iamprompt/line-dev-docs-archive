You can now change the order in which services are displayed on the [Provider page](/docs/partner-docs/provider-page/).

The provider page can be configured from the **Provider page** tab on the [LINE Developers Console](/console/). From the **Provider page** tab, drag and drop each service up and down to reflect the order in which they are displayed on the provider page.

You cannot change the order of the service categories: LINE Official Account, LINE MINI App, and LINE Login. You can only change the order in which the services under each category are displayed.

For details, see [Provider page](/docs/partner-docs/provider-page/) in the Options for corporate customers.
