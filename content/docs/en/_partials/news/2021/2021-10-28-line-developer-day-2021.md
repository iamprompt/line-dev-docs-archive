The tech conference, LINE DEVELOPER DAY 2021, is being held over 2 days, from November 10th until the 11th. All sessions will be delivered in three languages: Japanese, English and Korean.

More than 50 sessions will be delivered during LINE DEVELOPER DAY 2021. Among them, here are the sessions related to :glossary-tooltip[[LINE API](/glossary/#line-api)]{glossary-id="line-api"} and [LINE Developers site](/).

- [DAY2 15:40 - 16:00 Introduction to LIFF/LINE Mini App - Characteristics and Practical Development](https://linedevday.linecorp.com/2021/en/sessions/151/){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"}
- [DAY2 16:00 - 16:20 For Improvement of Developer Experience of All LIFF App Developers](https://linedevday.linecorp.com/2021/en/sessions/142/){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"}
- [DAY2 16:40 - 17:20 Specialized Technical Writing Team to Provide Documentation for LINE Developers](https://linedevday.linecorp.com/2021/en/sessions/148/){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"}

For more information about how to apply, the time of the event, and the content of the sessions, see [LINE DEVELOPER DAY 2021 official website](https://linedevday.linecorp.com/2021/en/){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"}.
