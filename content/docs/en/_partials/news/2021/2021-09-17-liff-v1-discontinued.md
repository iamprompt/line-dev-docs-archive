As announced on [April 5, 2021](/news/2021/04/05/liff-v1-deprecated/), LIFF v1 will be :glossary-tooltip[[end-of-life](/glossary/#end-of-life)]{glossary-id="end-of-life"} on October 1, 2021, which marks the end of its :glossary-tooltip[[deprecation](/glossary/#deprecated)]{glossary-id="deprecated"} period.

### Target version

LIFF v1

::admonition
---
title: If you're using LIFF v1, migrate to LIFF v2
type: warning
---
For more information on migrating to LIFF v2, see [Migrate to LIFF v2](/news/2021/04/05/liff-v1-deprecated/#migrate-to-v2) in the news from [April 5, 2021](/news/2021/04/05/liff-v1-deprecated/).
::

### Scheduled date of discontinuation

October 1, 2021

### Impact

Gradually after the discontinuation of LIFF v1 on October 1, 2021, you won't be able to refer to the LIFF SDK URL (`https://d.line-scdn.net/liff/1.0/sdk.js`) or use the [LIFF v1 API](/reference/liff-v1/).

LINE will continue to improve the quality of its services for its customers. Thank you for your understanding.
