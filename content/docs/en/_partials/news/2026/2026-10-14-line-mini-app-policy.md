As announced on [September 14, 2026](/news/2026/09/14/line-mini-app-policy/), the [LINE MINI App Policy](https://terms2.line.me/LINE_MINI_App?lang=en){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"} has been revised on October 14, 2026.

### Date of revision

October 14, 2026

### Details of revision

- Clarification of the conditions for serving ads within LINE MINI Apps
- Other formatting-related updates

### Background of the revision

To improve the safety of ads served within LINE MINI Apps and prevent fraudulent or malicious ads, we've clarified that, in principle, LY Ads Network is the only ad network platform that can be used.

We've also clarified the rules that apply to ads served through LY Ads Network, ads placed directly by service providers, and direct-sold ads served under contracts with advertisers.

We'll continue our efforts to provide an environment where users can use LINE MINI Apps safely and securely. Thank you for your understanding.
