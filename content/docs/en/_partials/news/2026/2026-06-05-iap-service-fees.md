The LINE MINI App [in-app purchase](/docs/line-mini-app/in-app-purchase/overview/) feature is offered free of charge until the end of June 2026 so that more customers can use it. From July 1, 2026, the applicable service fees will be charged.

With the introduction of service fees, we'll revise the [LINE In-App Purchase Terms of Use (for LINE MINI App Provider)](https://terms2.line.me/LINE_MINI_App_IAP?lang=en){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"}.

### Fee effective date

From payments processed on or after July 1, 2026, the applicable fees will be charged.

The fee rate applied will be the rate specified at the time of your application.

### Scheduled revision date

July 1, 2026

The content and date are subject to change.

### Details of revision

Main revisions:

- Clarifying descriptions based on the methods of calculating fees and of settlement/payment
- Other formatting changes
