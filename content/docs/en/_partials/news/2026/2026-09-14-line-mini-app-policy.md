[LINE MINI App Policy](https://terms2.line.me/LINE_MINI_App?lang=en){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"} will be revised on October 14, 2026.

### Scheduled date of revision

October 14, 2026

The content and date are subject to change.

### Details of revision

- Clarification of the conditions for serving ads within LINE MINI Apps
- Other formatting-related updates

The differences between before and after the revision are as follows:

| Before the revision (current)                                                              | After the revision                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [LINE MINI App Policy](https://terms2.line.me/LINE_MINI_App?lang=en){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"} | [LINE MINI App Policy](https://workers-hub.ent.box.com/s/cdtit2yzye5hhvmrbf7uzp6c8b1kixtw){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"} |

### Background of the revision

To improve the safety of ads served within LINE MINI Apps and prevent fraudulent or malicious ads, we'll clarify that, in principle, LY Ads Network is the only ad network platform that can be used.

We'll also clarify the rules that apply to ads served through LY Ads Network, ads placed directly by service providers, and direct-sold ads served under contracts with advertisers.

We'll continue our efforts to provide an environment where users can use LINE MINI Apps safely and securely. Thank you for your understanding.
