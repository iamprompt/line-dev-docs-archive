As announced on [February 6, 2026](/news/2026/02/06/line-mini-app-policy/), the [LINE MINI App Policy](https://terms2.line.me/LINE_MINI_App?lang=en){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"} has been revised on February 19, 2026.

### Date of revision

February 19, 2026

### Details of revision

- Changes related to digital content
- Clarifications to the privacy policy
- Other formatting-related updates

### Background of the revision

Previously, the sale of virtual items such as digital content was generally prohibited in LINE MINI Apps. However, starting today in Japan, virtual items can now be sold by using the [in-app purchase](/docs/line-mini-app/in-app-purchase/overview/) feature, which has become available. Accordingly, the LINE MINI App Policy has been revised.

We'll continue to work to further improve our service to our customers. Thank you for your understanding.
