::

Due to a technical issue, we've temporarily suspended part of the feature for using multiple LINE Official Accounts with the [add friend option](/docs/line-mini-app/service/add-friend-option/) in LINE MINI Apps.

We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience that this may cause.

### Temporarily suspended feature

The feature to [switch the LINE Official Account displayed on the channel consent screen](/docs/line-mini-app/service/add-friend-option/#channel-consent-screen)

### Impact

While the feature is suspended, adding the `prompt_bot_id` query parameter to a :glossary-tooltip[[LIFF URL](/glossary/#liff-url)]{glossary-id="liff-url"} or :glossary-tooltip[[permanent link](/glossary/#permanent-link-liff)]{glossary-id="permanent-link-liff"} won't switch the LINE Official Account displayed on the channel consent screen to the specified account.

You can continue to use the features to [switch the LINE Official Account displayed on the verification screen](/docs/line-mini-app/service/add-friend-option/#verification-screen) and to [switch the LINE Official Account displayed in the subwindow that prompts users to add or unblock the account](/docs/line-mini-app/service/add-friend-option/#subwindow).

### Resumption date

Early November 2026

The resumption date is subject to change without notice.

We sincerely apologize again for any inconvenience this may cause.
