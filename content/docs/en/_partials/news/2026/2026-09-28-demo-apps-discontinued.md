We will discontinue the following demo apps available on the LINE Developers site on September 30, 2026:

- Messaging API demo
- LINE Login demo
- LINE MINI App demos

  - Store reservation demo
  - Table order demo
  - Membership card demo
  - Event experience demo
  - Mobile experience demo
  - Purchase experience demo
  - Travel experience demo

Before discontinuing the demo apps, we removed the corresponding pages and sections from the LINE Developers site on September 28, 2026.

We will continue to work to further improve our services to our customers. Thank you for your understanding.
