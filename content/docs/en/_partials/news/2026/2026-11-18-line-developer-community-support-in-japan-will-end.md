LY Corporation has decided to discontinue its operational support for LINE Developer Community in Japan at the end of 2026.

At the initiative of LINE Corporation, LINE Developer Community in Japan began in February 2019 as the official developer community for LINE APIs. We'd like to thank all the developers once again for their contributions to promoting the use of LINE APIs through their active involvement in the community.

We understand that LINE Developer Community will change its name to L-Developer Community and continue its activities independently, led by volunteer developers. We wish the community continued success as a place where developers can learn from one another and connect.

We received the following message from Kazumasa Yamamoto, founder of L-Developer Community.

::admonition
---
title: Kazumasa Yamamoto, founder of L-Developer Community

Seven years ago, when I started using LINE APIs at work, this community was there to help me. What I do today is possible thanks to the support of LY Corporation and the contributions of so many people. I'm deeply grateful to all of you.

With help from the official documentation and AI, we now encounter fewer difficulties when using the APIs. Going forward, "what to create" will become even more important.

Starting in 2027, we'll continue as L-Developer Community, run by volunteers. With the three Ls of Learn, Level up, and Launch at our core, we'll continue to be a place where we can share the joy of creating and help one another. I'd love for you to join us on Discord!
