As of March 11, 2026, anyone who is a permitted customer in the [LINE MINI App Policy](https://terms2.line.me/LINE_MINI_App?lang=en){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"} can create a LINE MINI App channel for services in Taiwan or Thailand.

As a result, LINE MINI Apps whose region to provide the service is Taiwan or Thailand can now be published as :glossary-tooltip[[unverified MINI Apps](/glossary/#unverified-mini-app)]{glossary-id="unverified-mini-app"}.

For more information, see [Create a LINE MINI App Channel](/docs/line-mini-app/develop/develop-overview/#create-line-mini-app-channel) in the LINE MINI App documentation.

### Date of change

March 11, 2026

### Regarding verification review for LINE MINI Apps

If the region to provide the service is Taiwan or Thailand, only LINE MINI App channels under a certified provider can apply for the verification review to publish the LINE MINI App as a :glossary-tooltip[[verified MINI App](/glossary/#verified-mini-app)]{glossary-id="verified-mini-app"}.

For more information, see [Submitting LINE MINI App](/docs/line-mini-app/submit/submission-guide/) in the LINE MINI App documentation.

We'll continue to improve the quality of our products and developer documentation to make it easier for developers to create services using the LINE Platform.
