We've released a roadmap of upcoming features and the release dates for the [LINE Login](/docs/line-login/overview/), as well as for the [LINE Front-end Framework (LIFF)](/docs/liff/overview/) and the [LINE MINI App](/docs/line-mini-app/discover/introduction/), which use the system provided by the LINE Login.

- [Roadmap](/docs/line-login/roadmap/)

In the future, we plan to provide a means for submitting requests to the development team for features listed in the roadmap and for the products. Details will be announced as they are finalized.
