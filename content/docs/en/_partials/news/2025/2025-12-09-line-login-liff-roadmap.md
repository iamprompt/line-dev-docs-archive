As of December 9, 2025, we've discontinued publishing the roadmap for [LINE Login](/docs/line-login/overview/) and [LINE Front-end Framework (LIFF)](/docs/liff/overview/).

Going forward, information regarding feature additions and updates will be announced via [News](/news/).

We'll continue to improve the quality of the LINE Developers site and developer documentation to make it even easier for developers to develop services using the LINE Platform.
