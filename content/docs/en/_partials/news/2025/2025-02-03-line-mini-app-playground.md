We've released a demo app, [LINE MINI App Playground](https://miniapp.line.me/lineminiapp_playground){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"}, which allows developers to easily try out the features of the LINE MINI App.

![](/media/news/2025/line-mini-app-playground.png){className="[\"border\",\"w-fix-320\"]"}

By opening the LINE MINI App Playground on a smartphone with the LINE app installed, you can actually try out some of the features of the LINE MINI App. For example, you can try out features that are only available to [verified MINI Apps](/docs/line-mini-app/discover/introduction/#verified-mini-app), such as [adding a shortcut to your LINE MINI App to the home screen of a device](/docs/line-mini-app/develop/add-to-home-screen/) or [skipping the channel consent process](/docs/line-mini-app/develop/channel-consent-simplification/).

For more information, see [LINE MINI App Playground](https://miniapp.line.me/lineminiapp_playground){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"}.
