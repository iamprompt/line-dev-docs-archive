We plan to revise the "[LINE MINI App Platform Terms of Use](https://terms2.line.me/LINE_Developers_MINI_App_JP?country=JP&lang=en){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"}" for Japan on November 20, 2025.

### Scheduled date of change

November 20, 2025

The details and date of this revision are subject to change.

### Changes

The "LINE MINI App Platform Terms of Use" for Japan are scheduled to be revised as follows:

- Article 1, Section 5 will be changed
- Article 3, Section 3 before the revision will be deleted

The terms of use before and after the revision are as follows:

### Background

This revision aims to introduce more users to LINE MINI Apps of various service providers. It will expand opportunities for service growth.
