We'd like to inform you about a service outage regarding the [user account linking](/docs/messaging-api/linking-accounts/) feature using the Messaging API and LINE Login. The issue has now been resolved but we sincerely apologize for any inconvenience that this may have caused.

### Date and time

October 15, 2025 around 11:47 - October 21, 2025 around 15:51 (UTC+9)

### Cause of the outage

Server failure

### Impact

[User account linking](/docs/messaging-api/linking-accounts/)

### Details

There were cases that the process of user account linking failed in the :glossary-tooltip[[external browser](/glossary/#external-browser)]{glossary-id="external-browser"}.

  
We'll continue to improve the quality of its services to prevent future outages. Thank you for your understanding.
