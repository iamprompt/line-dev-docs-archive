In LINE version 15.18.0, **Favorites** has been added to the [options](/docs/line-mini-app/discover/builtin-features/#multi-tab-view-option) in the [multi-tab view](/docs/line-mini-app/discover/builtin-features/#multi-tab-view) of the LINE MINI App. Tapping **Favorites** adds the current LINE MINI App to the user's favorites.

![](/media/news/2025/mini-multi-tab-view-favorites-en.png){className="[\"border\",\"w-fix-320\",\"border\"]"}

LINE MINI Apps that have been added to the user's favorites can be viewed in the Wallet tab of the LINE app. This makes it easier for users to access the LINE MINI Apps that they use frequently.

### Requirements

**Favorites** is only available if all of the following conditions are met:

- The LINE MINI App is a [verified MINI App](/docs/line-mini-app/discover/introduction/#verified-mini-app).
- The user is located in Japan.
- The user's LINE version is 15.18.0 or later.
