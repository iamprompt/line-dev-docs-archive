In LINE version 15.20.0 or later, the behavior when minimizing the LINE MINI App and the LIFF app has changed. As the main update, the background plate is removed from the minimized icon display, and the icon appears larger than before. In addition, the method for closing the LINE MINI App and the LIFF app after they are minimized has also been changed.

### Target versions

LINE version 15.20.0 or later

### Changes

The main changes are as follows:

- [Icon size](#icon-size-20251204)
- [Behavior when minimized for the first time](#minimized-for-the-first-time-20251204)
- [Default position where the icon appears](#default-position-20251204)
- [Keep icon position](#keep-icon-position-20251204)
- [Closing method of minimized LINE MINI App/LIFF app](#closing-method-20251204)

#### Icon size

In versions of LINE earlier than 15.20.0, the icons of LINE MINI App and LIFF app are displayed on a rounded-square background plate.

Starting from LINE version 15.20.0, the background plate has been removed, and the icons are displayed larger than before. This improves the visibility of brand logos and other elements.

| LINE version earlier than 15.20.0                                     | LINE version 15.20.0 or later                                         |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ![](/media/news/2025/icon-size-asis.png){className="[\"w-fix-300\"]"} | ![](/media/news/2025/icon-size-tobe.png){className="[\"w-fix-300\"]"} |

#### Behavior when minimized for the first time

When a LINE MINI App or LIFF app is minimized, a transition message "Tap to open" appears for a few seconds only on the first occurrence. This message is displayed only once on the first minimization. From the second minimization onwards, the message won't be displayed.

![](/media/news/2025/minimized-for-the-first-time-en.png){className="[\"w-fix-300\"]"}

#### Default position where the icon appears

In versions of LINE earlier than 15.20.0, when a LINE MINI App or LIFF app was minimized, the minimized icon was displayed at the upper-right edge of the screen.

Starting from LINE version 15.20.0, the default display position has been changed to the middle-right edge of the screen.

| LINE version earlier than 15.20.0                                                 | LINE version 15.20.0 or later                                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ![](/media/news/2025/default-icon-position-asis.png){className="[\"w-fix-300\"]"} | ![](/media/news/2025/default-icon-position-tobe.png){className="[\"w-fix-300\"]"} |

#### Keep icon position

In LINE version 15.20.0 or later, when a minimized LINE MINI App or LIFF app is moved on the screen, its position is saved. The next time it's minimized, the icon appears at the saved position. Furthermore, if another LINE MINI App or LIFF app is opened and minimized within the same LINE app, it will also be displayed at the same position.

![](/media/news/2025/icon-position.png){className="[\"w-fix-720\"]"}

#### Closing method of minimized LINE MINI App/LIFF app

In versions of LINE earlier than 15.20.0, to close a minimized LINE MINI App or LIFF app, users had to drag and drop it onto the close button at the bottom of the screen or drag and swipe it out of the screen (iOS only).

Starting from LINE version 15.20.0, these previous closing methods have been removed. Instead, users can now close the minimized LINE MINI App or LIFF app more intuitively by tapping the close button displayed at the top-right corner of the minimized icon.

![](/media/news/2025/icon-with-close-button.png){className="[\"w-fix-300\"]"}
