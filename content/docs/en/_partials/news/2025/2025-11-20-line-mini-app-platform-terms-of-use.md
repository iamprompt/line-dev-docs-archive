As announced on [October 20, 2025](/news/2025/10/20/line-mini-app-platform-terms-of-use/), we revised the "[LINE MINI App Platform Terms of Use](https://terms2.line.me/LINE_Developers_MINI_App_JP?country=JP&lang=en){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"}" for Japan on November 20, 2025.

### Date of change

November 20, 2025

### Changes

The "LINE MINI App Platform Terms of Use" for Japan were revised as follows:

- Article 1, Section 5 was changed
- Article 3, Section 3 before the revision was deleted

See the revised "LINE MINI App Platform Terms of Use" at the following links:

| --------------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Japan                 | Japanese | [LINEミニアプリプラットフォーム規約](https://terms2.line.me/LINE_Developers_MINI_App_JP?country=JP&lang=ja){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"}                 |
| Japan                 | English  | [LINE MINI App Platform Terms of Use](https://terms2.line.me/LINE_Developers_MINI_App_JP?country=JP&lang=en){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"} |
