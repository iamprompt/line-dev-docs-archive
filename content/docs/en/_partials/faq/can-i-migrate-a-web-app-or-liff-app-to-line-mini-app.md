You can convert a web app into a LINE MINI App. For more information, see [Implementing web apps in operation as LINE MINI Apps](/docs/line-mini-app/develop/web-to-mini-app/) in the LINE MINI App documentation.

However, LIFF apps created under a LINE Login channel can't be migrated to a LINE MINI App channel to become either an unverified MINI App or a verified MINI App. If you're unsure whether to create a LIFF app or a LINE MINI App, or if the app may become a verified MINI App in the future, we recommend creating an unverified MINI App.
