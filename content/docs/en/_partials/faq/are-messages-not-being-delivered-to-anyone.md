No. The message isn't delivered to anyone.

If an error is returned when sending a [multicast message](/reference/messaging-api/#send-multicast-message), [narrowcast message](/reference/messaging-api/#send-narrowcast-message), or [broadcast message](/reference/messaging-api/#send-broadcast-message) via the Messaging API, the message isn't sent to any of the intended recipients.
