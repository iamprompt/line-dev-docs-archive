Yes, you can use a custom icon that you made for the LINE Share button. For information on how to set your own button icon, see the [Using custom icons](/docs/line-social-plugins/install-guide/using-line-share-buttons/#using-custom-icons).

When designing your button icon, make sure to refer to the [design guide](/docs/line-social-plugins/resources/design-guide/).
