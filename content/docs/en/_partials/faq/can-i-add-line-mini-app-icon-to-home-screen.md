Yes. Tapping **Add to Home** option after opening the dropdown menu from the [action button](/docs/line-mini-app/discover/builtin-features/#action-button) or using the [`liff.createShortcutOnHomeScreen()`](/reference/liff/#create-shortcut-on-home-screen) method will add a shortcut to your LINE MINI App to the home screen of the user's device. For services that users access frequently, such as membership cards and takeout orders, users can reopen the LINE MINI App from the shortcut icon without opening the LINE app first.

The feature for adding a LINE MINI App shortcut to the home screen is available only for verified MINI Apps. For more information, see [Add a shortcut to your LINE MINI App to the home screen of the user's device](/docs/line-mini-app/develop/add-to-home-screen/) in the LINE MINI App documentation.
