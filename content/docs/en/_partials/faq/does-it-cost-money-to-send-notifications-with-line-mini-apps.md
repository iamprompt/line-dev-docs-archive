With LINE MINI Apps, you can use service messages to send notifications required for providing the service at no cost. The service messages feature is available only for verified MINI Apps.

For more information, see [Sending service messages](/docs/line-mini-app/develop/service-messages/) in the LINE MINI App documentation.
