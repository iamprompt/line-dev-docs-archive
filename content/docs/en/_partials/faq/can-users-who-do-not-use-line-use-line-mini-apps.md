When users who haven't installed the LINE app open a LINE MINI App, the LINE MINI App service can be provided through a web browser.

For example, if travelers from countries or regions where the LINE app isn't widely used want to access a LINE MINI App service, they can open the service in a web browser without installing the LINE app.

For more information on enabling users who haven't installed the LINE app to access LINE MINI Apps, see [Open a LINE MINI App in an external browser](/docs/line-mini-app/develop/external-browser/) in the LINE MINI App documentation.
