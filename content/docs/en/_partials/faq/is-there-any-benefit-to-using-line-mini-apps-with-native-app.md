Yes. Because LINE MINI Apps have a low barrier to entry, they can serve as an entry point for new customers to try your service and can encourage membership registration and continued engagement. For customers who already use the service regularly, you may continue operating a native app.

For example, if you provide a membership card service through a native app, installing the app and completing membership registration may be a significant hurdle for users. With LINE MINI Apps, users can issue a membership card in just a few taps, or you can eliminate the need for membership registration. In this way, LINE MINI Apps can introduce users to your service and encourage continued engagement.
