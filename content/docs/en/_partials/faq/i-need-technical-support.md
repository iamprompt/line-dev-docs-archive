There is no technical support desk for individual developers. See [Documentation](/docs/) for information on the development process and specifications of the LINE APIs. You can also exchange information with other developers on the [Developers Community](/community/).

If you are a corporate customer and need technical support for LINE APIs, contact our technology partners for technical support:

- [Technology Partners (Japan)](https://www.lycbiz.com/jp/partner/technology/line/){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"}
- [Technology Partners (Thailand)](https://linedevth.line.me/th/tech-partner){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"}
- [Technology Partners (Taiwan)](https://tw.linebiz.com/partner/tech-partner/#bar-1){rel="[\"nofollow\"]"}

Any inquiry regarding the LINE APIs should be through our Technology partners.
