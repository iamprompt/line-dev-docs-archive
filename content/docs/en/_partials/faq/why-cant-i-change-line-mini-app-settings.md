Check whether the status of your LINE MINI App channel is "Reflected". While the channel is in this status, you can't change some settings or resubmit the app for review.

To change the settings, click the **Search enable** button on the **Review request** tab in the [LINE Developers Console](/console/). Once search is enabled for your LINE MINI App, the channel status returns to "Not yet reviewed", allowing you to change the settings and resubmit the app for review.

For more information, see [When submitting your LINE MINI App for the first time](/docs/line-mini-app/submit/submission-guide/#first-time) in the LINE MINI App documentation.
