A pioneering force in Thailand's LINE ecosystem, Sitthi Thiammekha was certified as one of the nation's very first LINE API Experts in 2018. He possesses deep, hands-on expertise across the entire LINE Platform, including the Messaging API, Flex Messages, LIFF, Rabbit LINE Pay, and the latest LINE MINI App.

Known for rapidly embracing new LINE features, Sitthi quickly developed a "World Cup LINE Bot" using the Flex Message API, demonstrating cutting-edge interactive chatbot capabilities. More recently, he spearheaded the LINE MINI App Ignition Bootcamp, empowering numerous startups to accelerate their mastery of LINE MINI App from its inception.

Sitthi's passion for knowledge sharing is evident through his regular technical articles for the LINE Developers Thailand community and his impactful talks and workshops on advanced topics. His contributions have been pivotal in significantly expanding the LINE developer ecosystem in Thailand. This includes leading the development of multiple transformative LINE API projects that leverage the platform for innovative business solutions.

With nearly two decades in software, Sitthi, a Thai technology entrepreneur, evolved from a full-stack developer to a product strategist. Today, he's the visionary founder of Mekha Innovation, an AI Transformation company focused on business automation with a significant emphasis on LINE-powered solutions. Beyond product leadership, Sitthi is a highly sought-after speaker and coach, conducting tailored in-house AI training to demystify AI strategy, Automation, and leveraging the LINE API for business growth.
