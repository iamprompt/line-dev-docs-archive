Yuji Ueki is a Hobby Programmer / Financial DX Consultant.
While working as a barista, he learned programming by himself. After that, he moved to the financial industry and worked as a lead architect and product manager for various DX projects at MS&AD and MUFG Bank. He is currently working at a tech company as Associate Partner engaged in DX consulting for financial institutions.
He is also creating virtual assistants including LINE Bot and developing related technologies, focusing on the compatibility of high quality user experience and practicality through conversation with characters.
