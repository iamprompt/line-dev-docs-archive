Dr. Winn pursued his PhD in Computer vision and Machine learning from University of Caen Normany, France.
He has been working in the field of applied Machine learning and Data sciences for almost 10 years, for several organizations and companies such as Daimler AG, The French National Center for Scientific Research (CNRS), AirAsia, and Telenor.

He is now a group senior data scientist in Telenor. His role is to drive group initiative projects and support Telenor's 14 business units across Europe and Asia in analytics. Currently, he is in charged of developing chatbots for Telenor.

Outside Telenor, he has self-developed an AI chatbot called ‘Botnoi’. Botnoi has gained more than 1 million friends in LINE and 200k users in Facebook within less than a year (2017), and has won an international award from LINE, Japan for it’s Natural Language processing (NLP), technology. He is the founder of iBotnoi Limited, an AI & Chatbot solution company.
