Supakarn Laorattanakul (Prompt) is a full-stack developer with a specialization in Node.js. He graduated from Mahidol University with a degree in Information and Communication Technology. He is a skilled developer with experience in a variety of frameworks, including ReactJS, NextJS, VueJS, TailwindCSS, Express, NestJS, and more. He is also an expert in the LINE API ecosystem, including the Messaging API, LINE Beacon, LINE Official Notification (LON), LINE Front-end Framework (LIFF), and LINE Login.

In 2020, he won the LINE HACK 2020 Student category for developing NAMA, a LINE chat bot for group appointments. NAMA is a user-friendly chatbot that allows users to easily schedule group appointments. It is a valuable tool for businesses and individuals who need to coordinate schedules with multiple people.

Prompt is a talented developer with a passion for technology. He is always looking for new ways to use technology to improve people's lives. He is a valuable asset to any team and is sure to make a significant contribution to the field of software development.
