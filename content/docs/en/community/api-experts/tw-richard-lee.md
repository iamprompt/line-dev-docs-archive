Richard Lee is co-founder & CTO of iCook, the leading recipe sharing community in Taiwan & Hong Kong. Before iCook, he was also a founder of INSIDE, a Taiwan tech blog focusing on starutp & internet trends. He was listed as Forbes Asia 30 Under 30 in 2017.
LINE: dlackty

Richard loves new technologies like mobile, data, machine learning and infrastrucutre. During his day work, he leads the full stack engineering team, and he contributed to open source projects including CocoaPods, Fastlane, Three20 and many others in his spare time.

He's also a passionate speaker giving talks in RubyConf Taiwan / WebConf / RailsPacific / AWS Summit and user groups of different topics in Chinese and English.
