Punsiri Boonyakiat, also known as Beat, is a professional with over 5 years of experience in the tech industry, specializing in Data and AI. As the Data Engineer Lead, she has led key initiatives such as integrating data pipelines for AI/ML, and enabling data ingestion for training and retrieval by ML and Generative AI. She has also worked extensively with LINE technology, seamlessly integrating the LINE Messaging API with advanced Data and AI solutions to drive innovation and impact.

In 2024, at LINE THAILAND DEVELOPER CONFERENCE 2024, she presented The Future of Retail E-commerce with Gemini AI, demonstrating how Vertex AI and Gemini transform e-commerce through Retrieval-Augmented Generation. The LINE Chatbot featured functions like product inquiries, text-based searches, and image-based searches.

In 2021, she delivered a standout talk at LINE THAILAND DEVELOPER CONFERENCE 2021 on Applied Machine Learning in Running Assistance Bot, where she showcased a chatbot solution for virtual running events. The solution utilized the LINE Messaging API and Google Cloud tools such as Dialogflow, Cloud Vision API, and BigQuery.

In 2020, she was part of the team behind the development of the Krungthai Connext LINE chatbot, designed to enhance customer engagement. This innovative solution earned the prestigious LINE Thailand Award, recognizing its impact and advanced features.
