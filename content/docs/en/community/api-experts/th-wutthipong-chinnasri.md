Asst. Prof. Dr. Wutthipong Chinnasri (Nain) is a dedicated professional with expertise in Information Technology and Computer Education. He holds a Doctor of Philosophy in Computer Education from King Mongkut's University of Technology North Bangkok, a Master of Science in Information Technology from King Mongkut's Institute of Technology Ladkrabang, and a Bachelor of Science in Information Management (First Class Honors) from Rangsit University.

He currently serves as an Assistant to the President at Rangsit University and holds the academic position of Assistant Professor in Information Technology.

In 2023 and 2024, he was recognized as a LINE Certified Coach for API, reflecting his commitment to excellence and his passion for driving impactful results using LINE's technologies. He is proficient in various LINE APIs, including the LINE Messaging API, LINE Front-end Framework (LIFF), and LINE Beacon.

Beyond his professional accomplishments, Dr. Wutthipong has made significant contributions to the academic field. He is the author of the book Practical Building a Powerful LINE OA for Developers, a comprehensive guide for developers aiming to enhance their skills in creating powerful LINE Official Accounts using the LINE API. Additionally, he published a research paper titled Development of an Activity Attendance Management System through LINE Official Account, which focuses on utilizing LINE's platform to streamline activity management processes.
