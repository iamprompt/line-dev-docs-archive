## The most-read article was about opening LINE MINI Apps

The most-read article from August through October, 2026 was [What to know when "Open in LINE" or "Log in with LINE" doesn't work as expected](/tips/2026/05/07/line-launch-issue/). Since its publication in May 2026, the article has consistently attracted readers, earning it the top spot.

This article explains cases in which LINE doesn't launch when users tap the **Open in LINE** button while using a LINE MINI App, including the causes, OS-specific points to keep in mind, and possible workarounds.

Other articles covering a range of topics, including the Messaging API and LIFF, were also popular.
